# Product Definition v0.1

## Product thesis

LearnWard is a **learning motivation layer** that sits on top of learning already happening in the real world.

It is not primarily a curriculum platform, generic chore chart, or reward wheel app.

> **Core proposition:** turn real academic results into visible growth, mastery, goals, and meaningful family rewards.

## Primary users

### Buyer / administrator
Parent or guardian.

Needs:
- less repeated nagging about study,
- a fair way to recognize progress,
- bounded reward costs,
- low administrative overhead,
- evidence that the system supports learning rather than merely increasing app engagement.

### Primary user
Child approximately 10–15 years old, especially late-primary and middle-school learners.

Needs:
- visible progress,
- short feedback loops,
- goals they can influence,
- meaningful recognition of improvement,
- autonomy over rewards and challenges,
- a sense that effort leads somewhere.

### Beneficiary
The family as a whole.

Desired outcome: reduce conflict around studying while increasing learner ownership.

## Beachhead segment

Families where:
- the child is capable of learning but inconsistent or difficult to motivate,
- learning occurs across multiple sources such as school, tutoring, worksheets, books, and online tools,
- parents already use informal rewards, reminders, or negotiated privileges,
- the child responds to progression systems, games, collections, streaks, goals, or unlocks.

## Problem statement

Learning often has a long and opaque feedback cycle:

```text
Study today
  ↓
Wait
  ↓
Test later
  ↓
Receive score
  ↓
Wait
  ↓
End-of-term result
```

Entertainment products provide much shorter feedback cycles:

```text
Action
  ↓
Feedback
  ↓
Progress
  ↓
Reward
  ↓
Next action
```

LearnWard should shorten the learning feedback loop without turning learning into pure reward-seeking.

## Jobs To Be Done

### Parent JTBD

> When my child studies and takes tests, help them see progress and stay motivated so I do not have to continuously remind, threaten, or manually negotiate rewards.

### Child JTBD

> When I put effort into learning, help me see that I am getting better and give me meaningful choices about goals and rewards.

### Emotional outcomes

Parent:
> “My child is becoming more self-directed.”

Child:
> “I am getting better at this.”

## Value proposition

A result should not stop at:

```text
Math: 7.5 / 10
```

LearnWard should translate it into something like:

```text
Math — Algebra
7.5 / 10
▲ +1.1 above your recent baseline
🎯 Goal achieved
🏆 New personal best
Mastery 68% → 72%
+38 XP
+12.5 RP
```

The product should make improvement visible, not merely record grades.

## Positioning

### What LearnWard is

A **family learning motivation system** that understands learning results from many sources.

### What LearnWard is not

- a full curriculum,
- an LMS,
- an AI tutor first,
- a generic chores app,
- a reward-only app,
- a social network,
- a gambling-style spin product.

## Strategic distinction

Many education products use this model:

```text
We teach the child
      ↓
We measure the child
      ↓
We reward the child
```

LearnWard should support:

```text
The child learns anywhere
          ↓
LearnWard captures the result
          ↓
LearnWard understands the progress
          ↓
LearnWard turns progress into motivation
```

## Long-term product progression

The intended motivation transition is:

```text
“I study because I get rewards.”
              ↓
“I study because I want to level up.”
              ↓
“I want to beat my personal best.”
              ↓
“I want to master this.”
```

External rewards are an entry mechanism, not the desired final state.

## MVP boundary

The first MVP should prove one loop:

```text
Result entered
   ↓
Evidence verified
   ↓
Growth calculated
   ↓
Progress shown
   ↓
XP + RP awarded
   ↓
Child chooses spin or save
   ↓
Reward fulfilled
```

Do not build a large curriculum, pet world, AI tutor, marketplace, school integration layer, or social system before this loop is validated.
